Amenities

Côte d'Azur is built around a resort-level amenity offer that few residential addresses on the coast can match all reserved for residents, inside a private, gated complex.

Spa & Wellness Centre

A dedicated 597 m² Spa & Wellness Centre anchors Blok 1, featuring an indoor–outdoor infinity-edge pool that flows from a sheltered interior pool hall out onto a 190 m² open-air terrace with sun loungers, framed by natural stone walls and cascading greenery — a private retreat within the complex, open to residents year-round.

Landscaped Grounds

Over 1,178 m² of green terraces and gardens wind through the three blocks, connected by stone-walled pathways, a landscaped stream crossing, and mature olive groves preserved from the original hillside. Bougainvillea, lavender and native Mediterranean planting soften every approach road and courtyard.

Private Parking

105 indoor parking spaces across the basement levels of Blok 1, plus 6 open spaces — a total of 111 spaces for 101 apartments, with dedicated accessible parking and private storage units available.

Arrival & Lobby

Each block has its own reception lobby and entrance with a covered drop-off, glazed stairwells and elevator access to every floor — designed as a quiet, hotel-standard arrival experience rather than a conventional apartment entrance.
