Location

Perched above the Adriatic in Bečići — footsteps from the coast, moments from Budva's marina and old town

Bečići The Heart of the Budva Riviera

Côte d'Azur is set on the olive-covered hillside directly above Bečići, home to the beach once awarded the Grand Prix in Paris (1935) as the most beautiful in Europe a nearly 2 km stretch of fine sand along a sheltered, warm-water bay, today holding Blue Flag status for its water quality.

Life at Your Doorstep

Budva's 2,500-year-old walled Old Town with its Venetian ramparts, waterfront restaurants and marina is a short drive or scenic coastal walk from Côte d'Azur along the seaside promenade. Beyond the beach, the surrounding hills offer hiking and cycling trails through olive groves and Mediterranean scrubland, while the Adriatic Highway connects the complex to the rest of Montenegro's coast from the fishing village of Rafailovići to the exclusive Sveti Stefan peninsula, a short drive to the south.

Elevated above the coastal road, Côte d'Azur enjoys the quiet of a residential hillside address while remaining within minutes of Bečići's restaurants, cafés, water sports and nightlife the best of both a private retreat and a resort lifestyle.
