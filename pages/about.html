About Us

Built for those who notice the details.

Côte d'Azur was conceived as an answer to a simple question: what does it mean to live well on the Montenegrin coast? Not a resort, and not a conventional apartment block, but a residence that treats the hillside itself as the architecture's main material — stepping the building down the slope in a series of green terraces and cascades, rather than flattening the land to fit it.

Design Philosophy

"Crafting spaces where vision meets precision — every line, every detail shaped with intention, designed to inspire and built to endure."

The architecture, developed by Lineare Project Solutions, favours a restrained material palette — natural stone, warm timber slat shading (brise-soleil), and light stone façades — so that the Adriatic Sea, the olive groves and the Bečići hillside remain the visual protagonists. Each block steps back as it rises, generating wide roof terraces and green rooftops rather than conventional balconies, so that the building reads as a series of terraced gardens as much as a residence.

Living walls of climbing greenery line the stream-facing façades; local stone gabion walls anchor the approach road and garden terraces; and every apartment is oriented to draw in cross-ventilation and natural light typical of the region's Mediterranean climate.

Why Bečići

Bečići's sandy beach was awarded the Grand Prix in Paris in 1935 as the most beautiful beach in Europe — and the bay it shelters remains, nearly a century later, one of the most sought-after addresses on the Montenegrin coast. Côte d'Azur sits above that bay, close enough to reach the promenade in minutes, high enough to keep the sea within view from almost every room.

The Investment Case

Montenegro continues to attract international buyers drawn by its Adriatic coastline, developing infrastructure and long-term growth potential. A residence at Côte d’Azur combines a rare hillside position above Bečići Bay with private resort-grade amenities, boutique residential scale and strong fundamentals in one of the country’s most active coastal markets.

The project also benefits from the experience behind Emerald Residence in Bar, a previous Emerald Invest residential development designed around practical layouts, family-oriented infrastructure and full lifestyle amenities.

Côte d’Azur is the next step in this development approach: more architectural, more private and positioned directly above one of the Budva Riviera’s most established beach destinations.
