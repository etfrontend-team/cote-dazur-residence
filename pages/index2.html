Introduction

Côte d’Azur Residence is a private hillside retreat in Bečići, designed around wellness, nature and panoramic Adriatic views.

A RESIDENCE DESIGNED AROUND WELLBEING

Private Spa and Wellness Centre

Indoor and outdoor swimming pools, sauna, steam room, massage rooms, gym and relaxation areas.

Landscaped Green Terraces

Layered gardens and planted terraces integrate the buildings into the natural hillside.

Panoramic Adriatic Views

The cascading architecture is designed to maximise natural light, privacy and open sea views.

Private Access and Parking

A dedicated approach road, drop-off zone, underground parking and private storage areas.

Minutes From the Coast

Close to Bečići Beach, Budva Old Town, the marina, restaurants and essential services.

The development brings together contemporary architecture, landscaped green terraces and a dedicated spa and wellness centre with indoor and outdoor pools. A private approach road crosses a natural stream, creating a calm arrival experience while keeping residents only minutes from the beach, Budva Old Town and the marina.
